❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a deposit?

Go to the Deposit page from the sidebar. You can deposit using a credit/debit card via Paystack or with cryptocurrency (USDT, BTC, ETH) via NowPayments. Minimum deposit is $500.

How do withdrawals work?

Withdrawal requests are accepted from the 1st to the 25th of each month. A 20% compliance tax applies. Payouts are processed on the last day of the month to your crypto wallet.

How does portfolio growth work?

Your portfolio generates growth automatically. The system applies compound growth periodically based on market performance. You can track your growth in real-time on the dashboard.

What is the referral program?

You earn 10% commission on deposits from members you refer. Share your unique referral code from the Profile page. Commissions are credited automatically.
